## Scratchpad

You have an append-only scratchpad: `scratchpad_write` adds a note and `scratchpad_read` returns your notes. Both are always available. Use it however you find useful, for example to record what you've found, your reasoning, or intermediate results. Notes can't be edited or deleted, so write a new note to correct an earlier one. Your notes are saved with the run and visible to the people reviewing it. They are not graded, and they are not your answer: submit that with `final_answer`.
